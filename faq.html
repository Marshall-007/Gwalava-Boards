Questions & Answers

The Things Customers Ask Us Most

New to buying board? Start here. If your question is not covered, call the shop or send us a message and we will talk it through with you.

A cutting list is simply a list of every panel you need, with the length, the width and how many of each. Add which edges must be banded and we can cut and edge the whole job for you. Bring it written down, send it by email, or send a photo of your sketch on WhatsApp - whatever is easiest.

No. We cut boards to size, so you can order only the panels your project needs rather than carrying a full sheet home and trying to cut it yourself. If your job does use most of a board, buying the full sheet is usually the better value - ask us and we will work out which way round is cheaper for you.

Edge banding is the strip glued onto the raw cut edge of a board. It hides the chipboard core, matches the colour of the face, and keeps moisture out - which matters a lot in kitchens and bathrooms. Any edge that will be seen or touched should be banded. We run an automatic edgebander, so the finish is neat and consistent.

We work with melamine faced board in a wide range of woodgrains and solid colours, along with matt finishes, worktops and plywood. You can see the range on our products page, and there are physical samples at the shop - colours always read differently on a screen, so it is worth coming to look before you commit.

Both. Plenty of our customers are builders and DIY installers who just want board cut, edged and ready to assemble. We also take on carpentry work - kitchens, wardrobes, TV units and desks. Have a look at the gallery to see the kind of work that comes out of our workshop.

It depends on the size of the cutting list and how busy the floor is on the day. Small lists are often turned around quickly; a full kitchen takes longer. Give us a call on +27 67 290 0119 with your list and we will tell you honestly when it will be ready.

Ask us when you place your order and we will let you know what we can arrange for your area and what it costs. Collection from the workshop in Eldorado Park is always an option - bring a bakkie or a trailer for anything longer than about a metre.

Send your sizes through the enquiry form, email gwalava@gmail.com, or phone the shop. The more detail you give us - sizes, quantities, colour and which edges need banding - the more accurate the quote comes back.
